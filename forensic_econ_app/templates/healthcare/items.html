Quick Guide: Adding Medical Costs

Add each medical cost item separately. Here's what each field means:

  • Item Label: Name of the medical service/item (e.g., "Physical Therapy")
  • Category: Type of medical cost for grouping in reports
  • Annual Cost: How much this costs per year
  • Growth Rate: Optional - how fast this specific cost increases (leave blank to use scenario default)
  • Start Year: When this expense begins (usually year 1)
  • Duration: How many years this cost continues (leave blank for lifetime)
  • Interval: How often the cost occurs (e.g., 1 = yearly, 2 = every other year)
  • One-time cost: Check for costs that only happen once
